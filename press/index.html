Press kit

Everything here may be used to write about Novena. Please keep the images unaltered.

In one sentence

Novena is a private, offline Catholic novena app for Android that helps you pray a nine-day novena with one gentle daily reminder, with no subscription, no account and no ads.

In a paragraph

Novena is a Catholic prayer app for Android built around one practice: the novena, nine days of prayer for one intention. It carries 23 novenas in each of five languages (English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian and Filipino), each language with its own catalog. Five novenas in each language are free with every feature, and the rest are a one-time unlock, never a subscription. It works offline, needs no account, shows no ads, and keeps everything a person prays on their own phone: there is no server of its own and no analytics. Every prayer shows its source.

Using the name

Please don't
