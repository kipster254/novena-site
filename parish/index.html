Novena

You found this through a card, poster or bulletin. Welcome. Novena is an independent app; it is not run by or affiliated with any parish or diocese, and whoever shared this card did so as a friend.

A novena is nine days of prayer for one intention. You can pray one right here, on this site: every day's full text is on the page. If you want help remembering, the Novena app for Android keeps your place and can remind you once a day. There is no account, no ads, and nothing you pray leaves your phone.
