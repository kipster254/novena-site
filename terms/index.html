2. What the app is

Novena is an offline app for praying novenas — nine-day sequences of Catholic prayer. It helps you find a novena, keep a written intention alongside it, see which day you are on, and receive one reminder a day at a time you choose.

The content is devotional and informational. The prayers, saint biographies and related text are provided for personal devotional use. They are not spiritual direction, and they are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Novena is not published by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the Catholic Church, any diocese, order or parish. Where a text carries an imprimatur or Nihil Obstat, the app displays it as part of that text's source information; a displayed imprimatur is an attribute of the source text and is not an endorsement of this app.

Some prayer texts in the app are original compositions written for it rather than reproductions of a traditional text of the same name. Where that is so, the app says so on that novena's own Sources sheet.

3. Your device, your data

Everything you create in the app — the novenas you start, your progress, your written intentions, your reminder times and your settings — is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server of ours, no backup and no sync.

This has one consequence you should understand before you rely on the app: if you delete the app, reset your device, or lose or replace your phone, your prayer history and your written intentions are gone permanently. We cannot recover them, because we never had them. Android's automatic backup to Google Drive is deliberately switched off for this app, so that nothing you write leaves your device. This is a deliberate trade for privacy, and it is not a defect.

Purchases are the exception: they belong to your store account, not to your device, and can be recovered — see section 5.

6. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your own device. There is no push service and no push token.

They depend on your device: notification permission, battery optimisation, Do Not Disturb, and the device being switched on. We cannot guarantee that a reminder arrives at a particular moment, or at all. Missing a reminder never affects your progress — the app never resets you and never sends a reminder to shame you into returning.
