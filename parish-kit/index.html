Parish kit

For anyone who wants to share Novena in their parish. Print this page: it comes out as a poster, a sheet of cards and a bulletin insert. Please ask your parish office before putting anything up or in the bulletin.

Not ready to print yet: the QR code and the web address will appear here once getnovena.app is live.

Bulletin insert (about 80 words)

Praying a novena? A novena is nine days of prayer for one intention. Novena, a free-to-start app for Android, holds your place and can send one gentle reminder a day, so day four doesn't slip. It has no ads, no account and no subscription, and what you pray stays on your phone. The full St. Jude novena is at kipster254.github.io/novena-site. (Novena is an independent app and is not affiliated with this parish.)

A note to hand to a priest or ministry lead

Father, or dear friend in ministry,

I have been using a small Catholic prayer app called Novena and wondered whether it might be worth a line in the bulletin. It helps people pray a novena for all nine days, with a gentle daily reminder. It has no ads, no account and no subscription, and nothing a person prays is sent anywhere. Every prayer in it shows its source.

It is an independent app, not affiliated with any diocese. The full text of the St. Jude novena is free at kipster254.github.io/novena-site, if you would like to read it first.

Thank you for considering it.
